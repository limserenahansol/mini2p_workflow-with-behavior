----- 3  the same test with two baselines - the sign flip
  baseline [ -2.0,  0.0] s  ->  mean over cells -0.0471 z,  Wilcoxon p = 0.004407   (SUPPRESSION)
  baseline [ -6.0, -4.0] s  ->  mean over cells +0.1196 z,  Wilcoxon p = 0.0008751   (EXCITATION)
  Same traces, same events, same post-window. Only the baseline moved, and the sign
  of the population result moved with it. That is why no pre-window version is reported.

----- 4  why an event-jitter null cannot be built here
  jitter of 3-15 s in either direction, 200 draws: 30.9 % of jittered events land within 1 s of a REAL delivery
  so the null contains the signal, is too narrow, and everything passes. The circular
  shift of the cell's own trace is used instead - it keeps the event times fixed.

----- 5  pin and heat were given in separate blocks
  pin     12.6 -  586.2 s   (53 deliveries)
  heat   269.2 -  517.8 s   (34 deliveries)
  pin deliveries inside the heat block [269, 518] s:  0
  pin, as the runs it actually is: 13-204 s (n=37);  519-586 s (n=16)
  So 'pin versus heat' is also 'early versus middle'. Permuting the 53/34 labels
  over the same delivery times mixes the blocks and cannot undo a confound with time.

----- 7  the global stimulus-free mask is biased late
  free frames: 41.1 % of the session
  mean time of the free frames     390.7 s
  mean time of the deliveries      293.6 s
  the reference therefore sits +97 s later than what it is a reference for
  inside the dense pin block [13, 204] s, only 11.2 % of frames are free
  A cell that drifts up over ten minutes is therefore scored against mostly-late quiet time,
  which looks like suppression during stimulation. TIER 1 uses the free frames within
  +-60 s of each delivery instead, so the drift cancels.

----- 8  how many deliveries have a genuinely clear pre-window
  pin    12 of  53 have 6 s with no other delivery before them
  heat    7 of  34 have 6 s with no other delivery before them
  with only those 12 pin trials, the smallest effect a cell could show and still
  clear its own shift null is 0.43 z (median over cells; 1.96 x null SD).
  the largest effect anything actually shows is 0.41 z, so this subset cannot resolve real responses.

----- 9  scoring coverage - what was actually scored, and for how long
  behaviour              episodes   frames  seconds
  Paw attending                 1        9      0.4
  Licking or biting             0        0      0.0
  Sustained lifting / guarding        0        0      0.0
  Escape / rearing              5       97      3.9
  reflex taps:  76 paw withdrawal,  50 flinch - these are instants, not episodes
  The held behaviours were tapped rather than held: licking and guarding were never
  scored at all, attending once. The reflex taps are the only substantial behaviour data.

===== what each number decided =====
  1, 8  ->  no pre-stimulus window is clean; per-delivery pin and heat responses WITHDRAWN
  2, 3  ->  a pre-window baseline inverts the population result; the AUC suppression WITHDRAWN
  4     ->  the jitter null is invalid; the circular shift is used instead
  5     ->  pin versus heat is confounded with time; modality-selectivity WITHDRAWN
  6     ->  pin, withdrawal and flinch are one finding, not four
  7     ->  the reference must be local; TIER 1 uses +-60 s around each delivery
  9     ->  only escape is testable among the held behaviours, and only as a hint
